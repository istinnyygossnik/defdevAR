# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [Bot Name] (“the Bot”) processes information when used through Discord.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on how the Bot is configured and used, it may process:

- **Discord information:** Discord user ID, username, message ID, channel ID, and message timestamp, where available and necessary for the Bot's operation.
- **Submitted content:** Messages containing player nicknames and IP addresses intended for the Bot's functionality.
- **Technical information:** Error logs and basic diagnostic information needed to maintain the service.

Please do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, or other unrelated sensitive information.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only as necessary to provide the Bot's stated functionality, including:

- Reading relevant messages in designated channels.
- Extracting submitted player nicknames and IP addresses.
- Passing relevant information to an authorized local automation tool.
- Preventing duplicate processing and diagnosing technical errors.
- Responding to support requests and maintaining service security.

The Bot is not intended to collect messages from unrelated channels or to build profiles of Discord users.

## 3. Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be transmitted to the local automation tool configured by the operator, where necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Information is not sold. It is not shared with unrelated third parties except where necessary to operate the service, where the user has appropriately directed the sharing, or where disclosure is required by law.

The operator is responsible for securing any external systems that receive information from the Bot.

The Bot should retain information only for as long as necessary for its stated functionality, troubleshooting, or applicable legal obligations.

Where information is stored, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information by contacting the operator at the email address below. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

Information that is no longer necessary will be deleted, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Children's Privacy
